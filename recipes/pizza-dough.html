Pizza Dough

Description

Pizza dough is a type of bread dough that is used to make pizza crust. It is typically made with flour, water, yeast, and salt, and can be topped with a variety of ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Bread Flour
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Let the dough come to room temparature
  2. Prepare your surface with oil
  3. Shape the dough
