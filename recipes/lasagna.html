Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a type of pasta that is made with wide, flat noodles and is typically layered with meat, cheese, and tomato sauce. It is a popular dish in Italian cuisine and is often baked in the oven.

Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Cheese
  • Eggs

Steps

  1. Cook the meat
  2. Cook the noodles
  3. Make the cheese layer
  4. Assemble the lasagna
  5. Bake the lasagna
